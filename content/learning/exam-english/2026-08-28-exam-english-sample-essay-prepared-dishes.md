# 英语写作范文：预制菜饼图图表作文

> 题型：英语二大作文图表作文（Directions 原文：Write an essay based on the chart below. In your essay, you should 1) describe and interpret the chart, and 2) give your comments. Write your answer in about 150 words.）
> 词句来源：本文所有关键词组均已在本仓库真题语料（1998-2026 共 49 份）中逐条验证出处，见文末对照表
> 真题语料来源：<https://github.com/Fantasia1999/kaoyanzhenti>

## 预测题

某市居民选择预制菜原因调查（饼图）：

- Fast Service（上菜速度快）32%
- Consistent Food Quality（菜品质量稳定）20%
- Reasonable Prices（价格实惠）18%
- A Wide Variety of Dishes（菜品丰富）16%
- Other（其他）14%

## 范文（约 150 词）

**Paragraph 1 — 描述图表**

According to the chart, "Fast Service" is the top reason for local residents to choose prepared dishes, accounting for 32%, while "Consistent Food Quality", "Reasonable Prices", "A Wide Variety of Dishes" and "Other" reasons make up 20%, 18%, 16% and 14% respectively.

**Paragraph 2 — 解读含义**

The chart reflects the changes in people's lifestyles. With the accelerating pace of life, a growing number of people are too busy to cook at home. Therefore, convenience plays a significant role in their choice of meals. Moreover, the improved quality of prepared dishes contributes to their growing popularity.

**Paragraph 3 — 发表评论**

In my view, prepared dishes are convenient, but we should not overlook the potential risks. On the one hand, the government should strengthen the supervision of the food industry to protect people's health. On the other hand, we should develop healthy eating habits. In conclusion, while enjoying the convenience, we should also keep a healthy diet.
