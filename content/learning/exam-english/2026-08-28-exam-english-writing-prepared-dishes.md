# 英语图表作文写作方法：预制菜主题

> 来源：微信公众号（顾不得）文章
> 链接：<https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/KB2LMnHXaoCWWwIcOM9MUw>
> 整理时间：2026-08-28
> 真题表达参考：<https://github.com/Fantasia1999/kaoyanzhenti>

图表作文常见要求是 "Write an essay based on the chart below... describe and interpret the chart, and give your comments"。考场上看到这样的题目，第一步是思考主题词的英文怎么写。

## 一、写英文主题词（三法）

**1. 直接法**：基础不错、知道写法时直接写

- `prepared dishes`（也可写作 `pre-made dishes`）

**2. 形容词 + 名词法**：不确定直接写法时，先想"预制菜 = ……的……"

- "提前做好的餐食" → `pre-cooked meals`（pre-cooked 为形容词，意为"预煮加工好的"）

**3. 过去分词作定语法**：会意译但不会写 pre-cooked 这个形容词时

- "预制菜 = 提前做好的菜" → `dishes prepared in advance`（prepared in advance 是过去分词短语，作 dishes 的后置定语）

## 二、写第一段（描述图表）

### 描述"选择原因"的词汇积累

| 中文 | 英文 |
|---|---|
| 上菜速度快 | fast service |
| 菜品质量稳定 | consistent food quality |
| 价格实惠 | reasonable prices |
| 菜品丰富 | wide variety of dishes |

### 三档句式（饼图 = 静态图，重点是介绍比例，按从大到小）

**初级：主谓宾直述 + while 从句**

> "Fast Service" is the top reason for local residents to choose prepared dishes, accounting for 32%, while "Consistent Food Quality", "Reasonable Prices", "A Wide Variety of Dishes" and "Other" reasons make up 20%, 18%, 16% and 14% respectively.

**中级：there be 句型 + 伴随状语**

> There are five main factors influencing local residents' choice of prepared dishes, with "Fast Service" taking the largest share at 32%, and the other four factors including "Consistent Food Quality", "Reasonable Prices", "A Wide Variety of Dishes" and "Other" ranging from 14% to 20%.

**高级：双重被动语态**

> "Fast Service" is recorded as the most decisive factor at 32%, followed by "Consistent Food Quality", "Reasonable Prices", "A Wide Variety of Dishes" and "Other" reasons in descending order.

## 三、写第二段（发表评价）

### 方法 1：记叙文写法

写作思路：时间 + 地点 + 人物 + 起因 + 矛盾 + 结局

> During my summer break from college, my parents were busy with work all day, and I struggled with what to cook for meals. At first, I was very skeptical of ready-to-cook meals, always thinking the ingredients weren't fresh enough. After cooking for several days in a row, I decided to give them a try and bought a few sets—only to discover that they tasted great and were very reasonably priced. Now, I often stock up on some ready-to-cook meals just in case.

### 方法 2：议论文写法（模板化，适合时间短、基础弱）

结构固定，可提前背诵：第一句交代主题词很流行 → 第二句说流行的背后有很多原因 → 第三、四句介绍具体原因（方便快捷、省钱……）

- 第一句：预制菜近期很流行
- 第二句：流行的背后有很多原因
- 第三句：第一个原因是方便快捷
- 第四句：第二个原因是省钱

## 四、写第三段（总结全文）

最简单：第一句总结上文，第二句推测未来。

> In short, prepared dishes are becoming increasingly widespread, and their growth momentum should not be underestimated. We are confident that in the future, prepared dishes will gain even broader recognition and popularity.

# 完整范文（预制菜饼图，约 150 词）

## 预测题

某市居民选择预制菜原因调查（饼图）：

- Fast Service（上菜速度快）32%
- Consistent Food Quality（菜品质量稳定）20%
- Reasonable Prices（价格实惠）18%
- A Wide Variety of Dishes（菜品丰富）16%
- Other（其他）14%

## 范文

**Paragraph 1 — 描述图表**

According to the chart, "Fast Service" is the top reason for local residents to choose prepared dishes, accounting for 32%, while "Consistent Food Quality", "Reasonable Prices", "A Wide Variety of Dishes" and "Other" reasons make up 20%, 18%, 16% and 14% respectively.

**Paragraph 2 — 解读含义**

The chart reflects changes in people's lifestyles. As the pace of life accelerates, a growing number of people are too busy to cook at home. Convenience therefore plays a significant role in their choice of meals. Moreover, improvements in food quality contribute to the growing popularity of prepared dishes.

**Paragraph 3 — 发表评论**

In my view, prepared dishes are convenient, but we should not overlook the potential risks. On the one hand, the government should regulate the food industry more effectively to protect public health. On the other hand, consumers should develop healthy eating habits. In conclusion, convenience should not come at the expense of a healthy diet.

## 值得借用的真题表达

这篇范文优先复用了历年真题中完整出现过、放进本文也自然的表达。下面只列真正值得背的几组，不做命中率统计：

| 表达 | 真题出处示例 |
|---|---|
| `the top reason` | 2018 英语一 |
| `accounting for` | 2016、2021、2026 英语二 |
| `a growing number of` | 2013、2023 英语一 |
| `plays a significant role` | 2026 英语二 |
| `contributes to` | 2017 英语一、2022 英语二 |
| `potential risks` | 2013 英语一 |
| `On the one hand ... On the other hand` | 2007 通用卷、2018 英语一 |
| `food industry` | 2011 英语二 |
| `eating habits` | 2022 英语二 |
| `In conclusion` | 2016 英语二、2024 英语一 |

`prepared dishes` 等主题词来自本次题目材料。真题语料负责提供自然的通用表达，不需要硬把每个主题词都从旧题里找出来。

## 使用说明

1. **背诵优先段落 2、3**：段 1 描述数据随题变化，段 2、3 是"万金油"句式，可套任意图表作文
2. **替换数据即可复用**：把 32%/20%/18%/16%/14% 换成实际图表比例，第一段即成
3. **表达表的用法**：优先记住可以迁移到其他作文里的通用表达，主题词则根据实际题目替换
